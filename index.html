💡 How this works in the physical lab:

In my physical Arduino prototype, each of the 7 cathode pins is mapped to digital pins D2–D8 via 220Ω current-limiting resistors. Two tactile buttons on pins D9 and D10 trigger debounced software interrupts to increment/decrement the counter, outputting bitmasks from a precomputed lookup array.
